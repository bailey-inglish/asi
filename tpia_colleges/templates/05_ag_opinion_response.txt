  1. FERPA Directory Information Exception: The Family Educational Rights and Privacy

  2. No Applicable TPIA Exception: None of the TPIA's specific exceptions (Government
     Code § 552.101–§ 552.159) shield directory information for students who have not

     participation, which is in the public interest.
